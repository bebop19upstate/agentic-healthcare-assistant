Management of type 2 diabetes typically begins with lifestyle changes including diet modification and increased physical activity, alongside metformin as a first-line medication for most patients. When metformin alone is insufficient, additional medication classes may be added, including SGLT2 inhibitors and GLP-1 receptor agonists, both of which have the added benefit of supporting cardiovascular and kidney health beyond blood sugar control. Regular monitoring of hemoglobin A1C, a measure of average blood sugar over three months, helps guide treatment decisions. Patients are generally advised to have an A1C target below 7% for good long-term outcomes, though this varies by age and individual health circumstances.
