Type 2 diabetes is a chronic condition affecting how the body processes blood sugar (glucose), caused by the body becoming resistant to insulin or not producing enough of it. Risk factors include obesity, sedentary lifestyle, and family history. Symptoms often develop gradually and can include increased thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, and blurred vision. Left unmanaged, type 2 diabetes can lead to serious complications including cardiovascular disease, nerve damage, vision loss, and kidney disease, making it one of the leading causes of chronic kidney disease worldwide.
